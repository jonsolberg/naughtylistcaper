The Naughty List Caper

A point-and-click Christmas grudge in six acts, one very cold boat ride and a sleigh

Walk to

This game plays best sideways, in full screen.

Pick a verb, then tap something in the scene or in your pockets. Right-click does the obvious thing: talk to people, pick up loose items, look at everything else. For "Use" and "Give", pick the item first. Tap anywhere to skip a line of dialogue. Progress saves automatically, and Save and Load at the top keep up to three games of your own.
